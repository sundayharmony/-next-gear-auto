# Hudson County NJ: Tickets on Rental/Leased Vehicles — Municipal Guide

Research date: 2026-11-22. Method: official municipal and njcourts.gov pages only (fetched/searched that day). Each fact is marked VERIFIED (URL) or UNVERIFIED (how to confirm). Wording is paraphrased.

## Key findings (read first)

1. **No joint courts in Hudson.** The Judiciary's court-sessions list shows 12 separate Hudson municipal courts (codes 0901-0912), one per town in this scope, none marked joint. VERIFIED: https://www.njcourts.gov/sites/default/files/courtsessionsbycty.pdf (undated PDF; confirm with each court). Caveat: East Newark's court is temporarily operating out of the Harrison courthouse (notice dated 2025-05-27; end date not stated). VERIFIED: https://www.boroughofeastnewark.com/News/View/2408/municipal-court-temporarily-operating-from-harrison-municipal-court-house
2. **Statute (the controlling rule).** N.J.S.A. 39:4-139.5: a lessor is not liable for a *parking* offense if the lessee had possession/control. To get that protection the lessor, after notice of the offense, must give the court clerk handling the case a sworn, notarized statement naming the lessee and the lessee's address, on a form prescribed by the Administrative Director of the Courts. No fixed day count is stated. The lessor need not attend the hearing unless told the offense may stem from the lessor's failure to maintain the car. If the lessor fails to comply, the court may take any action justice requires, including holding the lessor liable. VERIFIED: https://law.justia.com/codes/new-jersey/title-39/section-39-4-139-5/ (a secondary legal site; confirm current text at https://pub.njleg.gov/ or njleg.state.nj.us).
3. **No municipality published lessee guidance.** None of the 12 official town/court pages reviewed mention rental/lessee statements (every page checked said nothing on it). Nothing found for Jersey City either (confirms the user's note).
4. **No court-published lessor form found.** I did not find the Administrative Director's prescribed lessor/lessee form on njcourts.gov, and no town links one. Treat the form as UNVERIFIED. Confirm by calling AOC Municipal Court Services Division, (609) 815-2900 ext. 54850 (number appears on https://www.njcourts.gov/courts/municipal and https://www.kearnynj.org/municipal-court/), or asking each clerk which form they accept.
5. **Moving violations.** 39:4-139.5 covers parking offenses only (per the text read). Police moving tickets are normally issued to the driver at the stop; no town page describes owner/lessee handling for them. UNVERIFIED how each court handles a moving summons mailed to an owner (e.g., failure-to-appear notices); ask the clerk.
6. **Camera tickets.** Only two programs found: Hoboken "CLEAR" (cameras on Washington St; mailed to registered owner; the page says it ran 2025-10-01 to 2025-12-31, so status after that is UNVERIFIED) and a Jersey City camera-assisted parking pilot (announced; status, locations, contest process not stated). No camera programs found on the other towns' pages (absence on pages is not proof of none).
7. **Notarization for the statute is required by the statute text** (VERIFIED, source above). No municipal page states its own notarization rule, so in the table the cell reads "Yes per statute".
8. **Late fees/overdue.** Each notice adds at least $10 in Jersey City (VERIFIED, JC court page); West New York adds a state-ordered time-payment fee after the pay-by date; since 2024-10-01 license/registration suspensions for unpaid parking resumed (VERIFIED on Kearny, Bayonne, North Bergen, East Newark pages and https://www.njcourts.gov/notices/notice-municipal-courts-resumption-of-drivers-license-and-registration-suspensions-parking). For a rental company the practical risk is a registration suspension on the vehicle plate, so resolve promptly.

## Suggested workflow (practitioner, not legal advice)
1. On receipt of any parking notice, immediately call/email the issuing town's court clerk, say the car was leased, and ask: which form, whether the court accepts a company-drafted notarized statement naming the lessee, where to send it, and what the deadline is.
2. Send the notarized statement (lessee name and address, plus rental agreement and ID copy as supporting evidence, which is customary but not required by the statute) before the court date; keep proof of delivery.
3. Because a pay-by or court date will pass, never let it lapse: file first, then follow up.
4. For moving and camera tickets, ask the court whether the owner is the named defendant; if so, ask for the accepted lessee-identification procedure.
5. Consult a NJ attorney for edge cases (mechanical failure carve-out, 5-day lessee notice).
